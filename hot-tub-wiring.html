Questions

Hot tub wiring FAQ

What size circuit does a hot tub need?

It depends on the tub. Many need a 40 A to 60 A, 240 V circuit, and swim spas can need more. The spec sheet tells us exactly.

Where does the disconnect go?

The Canadian Electrical Code sets where the disconnect can go relative to the tub. We place it where it's compliant and easy to reach.

Can you wire a plug-in (120 V) tub?

Yes. Plug-in tubs need their own GFCI-protected outlet on a dedicated circuit. We'll check what you have and add one if needed.

Do I need a permit?

Yes, and we pull it. The work is inspected before the tub goes into use.
