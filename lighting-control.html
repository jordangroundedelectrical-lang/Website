Systems

Two systems, one installer.

Both work with the Lutron app and popular voice assistants and smart home apps.

Caseta

Wireless dimmers, switches and Pico remotes that replace your existing switches. A good fit for a few rooms, a renovation or a first step into smart lighting.

RadioRA

Lutron's whole-home system, with keypads, scenes and schedules across the house. A good fit for larger homes, renovations and new builds.

Questions

Lighting control FAQ

Do I need new wiring?

Usually not for Caseta, which is wireless and replaces your existing switches. RadioRA can also be retrofitted, though larger systems are easiest during a renovation or new build.

Will my LED lights dim properly?

Dimming depends on the bulbs and fixtures. We check compatibility before we install, so you don't get flicker or buzz.

Can you set up scenes and schedules?

Yes. We program scenes like "evening" or "away" and schedules, then show you how to change them yourself.
