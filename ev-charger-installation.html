Questions

EV charger FAQ

What size circuit does a home charger need?

It depends on the charger and your panel. Many home chargers run on a 40 A to 60 A circuit. We size the circuit to the charger and to what your service can carry.

Do I need a permit?

Yes. A new EV charger circuit needs an electrical permit. We pull it and book the inspection for you.

My panel is only 100 A. Do I need an upgrade?

Not always. A load calculation tells us, and load management can often let a charger share your existing service. If you do need more capacity, see service upgrades.

How long does an install take?

Most home installs are done in a day or less once we've seen the site. Long runs, a panel change or a service upgrade add time, and we'll tell you up front.
