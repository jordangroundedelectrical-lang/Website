Load management

Load management in Calgary: add the load, skip the upgrade.

Your panel may have more room than you think. We calculate what your service can actually carry, then install a load management system that shares that capacity, so an EV charger, hot tub or heat pump can often go in without a new service.

When it makes sense

Big new loads, same service.

A load management system watches how much power your building is using and slows, pauses or schedules a large load when the service gets busy. It is approved equipment, installed to code, permitted and inspected.

Adding an EV charger

On paper, many 100 A homes can't take a full-size charger circuit. An EV energy management system (EVEMS) slows charging when the oven, dryer and heat are all running, then speeds back up. Most charging happens overnight, so most drivers never notice.

Hot tubs and heat pumps

Large loads that run for hours. Load management can hold one load back while another runs, so they fit inside the service you already have.

Condos and parkades

Several chargers share one building circuit, with more added later as owners ask. See condo and apartment EV charging.

Load management or upgrade?

We'll tell you which one you need.

The load calculation decides. We don't sell an upgrade you don't need, and we won't push load management where a bigger service is the right call.

Load management often costs less than an upgrade, needs no utility shutdown, and keeps your existing panel and meter.

A service upgrade is the answer when the service can't carry the load even with management, or when you're planning bigger changes like a suite, a major renovation or several new loads at once.

Sometimes it's both: a modest upgrade now, with load management to stretch it further.

Questions

Load management FAQ

Is load management allowed by code?

Yes. The Canadian Electrical Code allows an approved energy management system to be counted in the load calculation, as long as it is installed to code. Every install we do is permitted and inspected.

Will my EV charge slower?

Only while the house is using a lot of power at the same time. Most charging happens overnight when the house is quiet, so most drivers don't notice a difference.

Will it work with my charger?

Some chargers have load management built in; others pair with a separate device. Tell us the make and model and we'll check before we give you a price.

When do I really need a service upgrade?

When the load calculation shows your service can't carry what you have plus what you're adding, even with load management, or when you're planning several big changes. See service upgrades.
