How it works

What upgrade day looks like.

  1. Site visit

    We check your service and run a load calculation, so the new service is sized for what you're adding.

  2. Firm price and permit

    You get a fixed price. We pull the electrical permit.

  3. Book the shutdown

    We coordinate the disconnect and reconnect with the utility ahead of time: ENMAX in Calgary, FortisAlberta in most surrounding towns.

  4. Upgrade and inspect

    Power off, new service in, inspected, and power back on. We walk you through the new panel.

Questions

Service upgrade FAQ

How long will my power be off?

For most homes it's part of a working day. We'll give you the window when we book the shutdown with the utility, so you can plan around it.

Do I need to call the power company?

No. We book the disconnect and reconnect with your utility (ENMAX in Calgary, FortisAlberta in most surrounding towns) and coordinate with them on the day. The utility handles the meter itself.

Do I need an upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. A load calculation tells us, and load management can often let a charger share your existing service.

Is the work warrantied?

Yes. Our workmanship is warrantied for 2 years, and so are the products we supply.
